Powerful Features

Everything you need to get things done.

Everything at a glance

The countdown to your day and where you stand on vendors, payments, guests and tasks. You see what's done and what's still to do.

Vendors and payments, in order

Compare options, confirm your vendors and keep track of every deposit and balance. The budget is always up to date, and you get a gentle reminder before each deadline.

Your guests, every detail

Replies, menus, allergies and dietary needs, grouped by household. The catering summary is ready to share as a PDF.

Tables that come together

Arrange the tables on the map and seat families together. On-device Apple Intelligence can suggest a seating plan, which you can then change as you like.
