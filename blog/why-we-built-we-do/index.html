Why we built We Do

When my girlfriend and I got engaged, we started planning right away. Within a few weeks everything was scattered. The budget was in a spreadsheet, vendor deposits were buried in chats, guest allergies sat in a notes app, and the seating plan was on a sheet of paper we kept redrawing.

We wanted one place that showed us where we stood, so we built it together while planning our own wedding.

We Do covers what we needed most:

  • Overview: a countdown and a quick look at what’s done and what’s next.
  • Vendors and payments: every deposit and balance tracked, with a reminder before each one is due.
  • Guests: replies, menus and allergies, plus a summary you can send to the caterer.
  • Tables: a seating map, with optional suggestions from Apple Intelligence that run entirely on your device.

It works on iPhone, iPad and Mac. We kept it calm on purpose, with just a few reminders and only for things that matter.

We made it for us, and we hope it makes planning a little easier for you too.

From the first yes to the big day, everything in its place.
