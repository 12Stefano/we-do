Privacy Policy for We Do

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the We Do app (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use it on iPhone, iPad or Mac. The App is published by Stefano Pozzi (“Developer”).

In short: We Do has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect your data and we cannot see it. Your wedding plan stays on your devices and, if you choose, in your own iCloud account.

1. Information you enter in the App

To help you plan your wedding, the App lets you enter information such as:

  • your names, wedding date, budget and currency;
  • vendors, prices, payments and payment deadlines;
  • your guest list, including household groupings, attendance, menu choices, allergies and dietary needs;
  • seating arrangements and tables;
  • checklist tasks and notes.

Some of this information, such as the allergies and dietary needs of your guests, may be considered sensitive. You decide what to enter. Please make sure you have a reasonable basis to record information about other people, such as your guests, and enter only what you need.

2. How your information is stored

On your device. Your information is stored locally in the App’s private storage on your device.

In iCloud (optional). If iCloud is available and enabled for the App, your information is synchronized between your devices through Apple’s CloudKit, in your private iCloud database. This data is tied to your Apple Account and is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. The Developer has no access to your private iCloud database and cannot read, copy or recover your data. If you turn iCloud sync off for the App, your data stays on the device only.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, receive, store or have access to your personal information. In particular, the App:

  • does not require you to create an account or sign in;
  • does not use analytics, crash-reporting or advertising tools;
  • does not track you across apps or websites;
  • does not sell, rent or share your information with third parties;
  • does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.

4. Notifications

The App may send you reminders, for example about payment deadlines or missing RSVPs. These are local notifications, created and delivered on your device. No notification content is sent to us or to any server. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device’s Settings.

5. Apple Intelligence

On devices that support it, the App may use Apple Intelligence through Apple’s on-device language model to offer suggestions, such as seating ideas, checklist ideas or a summary of your planning status. This processing happens entirely on your device. Your content is not sent to us or to any remote AI service by the App. Suggestions are optional, and the App works fully without them.

6. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, we cannot access, correct, export or delete it on your behalf. You stay in control of it:

  • Access and correction: view and edit your data directly in the App.
  • Deletion: delete items in the App, or delete the App. To remove data stored in iCloud, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage on your device, and delete the App’s data.
  • Portability: use the export features the App offers, such as the guest report in PDF.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region with data protection laws, you have rights under those laws, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Since we do not hold your data, you can exercise these rights directly in the App and in your iCloud settings. If you believe your rights have been violated, you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for adults planning a wedding and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The App may be used to record details about guests of any age, such as infants seated with their families. That information is entered and controlled by you and is not accessible to us.

8. Security

Your data is protected by the security features of your device and of Apple’s iCloud, including encryption. No system is completely secure, so we recommend keeping your device protected with a passcode and your operating system up to date.

9. Third-party services

The App uses only Apple’s system services (iCloud/CloudKit, local notifications and, where available, Apple Intelligence). It contains no third-party SDKs and no third-party advertising or analytics services. Apple’s handling of your data is governed by Apple’s own terms and privacy policy.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App adds new features. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest version. If a change significantly affects how your data is handled, we will highlight it in the App or in the App’s release notes.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Stefano Pozzi through our website.
